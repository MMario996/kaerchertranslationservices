Stay Updated!

Get notified automatically – right in Google Chat.

chat Google Chat Notifications

Translation-Services sends you automatic notifications when:

✓ Your project has been successfully submitted
✓ Your translation is ready to download
✓ A colleague shares a project with you
✓ A project deadline is approaching (24h reminder)
How to activate (3 steps):
1 Open Google Chat and search for the bot "Translation-Services"
2 Click on the bot and send any message (e.g. "Hi") – this registers your account
3 Click the tune icon here (top right) – set Notifications to ON – Save
• When do I receive the first message?
After setting Notifications to ON, you will receive your first message automatically when you submit your first project. No further action needed.
Note: The bot is read-only – it only sends notifications. It cannot execute commands or answer questions.
